ShopSmart Returns Policy

Electronics purchased from our store can be returned within 30 days of the original purchase date.
All returned electronics must include the original receipt, original packaging, and be in unused condition.
Damaged or opened electronics are not eligible for return unless they are defective.

Footwear and apparel can be returned within 60 days of purchase if unworn and with tags attached.
Refunds for returned items are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the return is received.
Shipped orders that the customer no longer wants must go through this returns process instead of cancellation.
